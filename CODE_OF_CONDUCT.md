# Code of conduct

We want UnboxAnyRepo to be a welcoming project for everyone who uses or contributes to it.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful and assume good intent; critique ideas and code, not people.
- Be patient with newcomers and with questions that have been asked before.
- Give and accept constructive feedback gracefully.
- Respect people's time and privacy.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, discrimination, insults or personal attacks of any kind.
- Sexualised language or imagery, and unwelcome attention.
- Publishing others' private information without permission.
- Trolling, sustained disruption, or deliberately derailing discussions.
- Using the project's infrastructure to attack others, or disclosing vulnerabilities publicly before they are fixed (see `SECURITY.md`).

## Scope and enforcement

This applies to all project spaces (issues, pull requests, discussions, chat) and to public spaces where someone represents the project.

Report concerns privately to the maintainers at `CONDUCT-CONTACT@EXAMPLE.COM` *(placeholder: replace before launch)*. Reports are handled confidentially. Maintainers may respond with a warning, temporary restrictions, or a permanent ban, proportionate to the behaviour.

Maintainers who would prefer an established text can adopt the Contributor Covenant (https://www.contributor-covenant.org) instead of this document.
